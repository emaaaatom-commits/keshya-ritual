Hair fall questions, answered

How much hair fall is normal?

Losing roughly 50 to 100 strands a day is commonly cited as normal shedding. If your hair looks clearly thinner, or you see bald patches, treat it as a problem worth checking.

How long until I see results?

Hair grows slowly, so give any routine at least 3 to 6 months. A product that promises results in days is overselling.

Is it dandruff or just a dry scalp?

Dandruff flakes are often larger and oily-looking, and the scalp may itch. Dry-scalp flakes are smaller and drier, and often come with dry skin elsewhere. Medicated shampoos help dandruff, but they can worsen a dry scalp.

Do oils and supplements really work?

Oils can reduce breakage and make the scalp feel better. Supplements like biotin mainly help if you are actually low in a nutrient, so check with a doctor before starting any.

When should I see a doctor?

See a doctor or dermatologist if hair loss is sudden, patchy, or follows illness, childbirth, or a crash diet, or if your scalp is red, painful or scaly. Causes like thyroid problems or low iron need testing, not just a shampoo.
